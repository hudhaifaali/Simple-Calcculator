# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple program to calculate simple interest based on the
principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Inputs
- Principal: Initial amount of money
- Rate: Annual interest rate in percentage
- Time: Time period in years

## Example
Principal = 1000
Rate = 5%
Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

## Output
Simple Interest = 100
